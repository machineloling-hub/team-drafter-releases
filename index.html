Install

  1. Run the installer. Windows may say the app is unrecognized the first time: click More info, then Run anyway.
  2. Open team_drafter. Create an account with your email and the code it sends, or sign in if you already have a machineloling account. Joe approves new accounts.
  3. On the Team tab, paste the drafter.lol link, click our side, and pick the seat you play.

What it does

  • Follows the drafter.lol draft live: bans, picks, whose turn it is.
  • Scores the board the way machineloling.gg does: the win model, synergies, matchups, your pool against the board, ban targets.
  • Links teammates on the same draft: everyone running the app on our side shares their account, mastery and pool.
  • Looks opposing players up by Riot ID: rank, mastery and recency on every champion.

Updates

There is no auto-update. When a new build is posted here, run the new installer over the old one.
